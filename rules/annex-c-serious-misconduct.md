# ANNEX C - Serious Misconduct

**C.1.** It is paramount that all members and licensees treat each other with
respect and consideration when interacting within the confines of The Club,
especially in front of members of the public.

**C.2.** Minor disagreements such as a difference of opinion is not considered
serious misconduct in the Club rules.

**C.3.** Actions that will be considered serious misconduct in the Club rules are:

a) Abusive, threatening or insulting behaviour (persistent in minor cases)
toward another person or business

b) Any criminal act, theft or assault

c) Knowingly acting as Pilot in Command without a current licence, medical
and/or insurance.

c) Persistently failing to carry out the requirements of any licence or
agreement without reasonable cause.

This is not an exhaustive list and is merely an aid to any future dispute. The
conduct MUST be serious, as considered by any reasonable person.
