# Expenditure and Payment Card Policy

> **Document status:** DRAFT
> **Document version:** 0.1 — 17/04/2026 — Jeff Helm
> **Next review date:**

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out how expenditure on behalf of Fenland Aero Club ("the Club") is approved, paid, recorded and reimbursed, ensuring proper financial control, transparency and VAT compliance.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all Committee members and any Club member authorised to incur expenditure on behalf of the Club.

## 3. Authority and Cards

### 3.1 Committee Authority

All expenditure must be approved by the Committee, either in advance at a Committee meeting (recorded in the minutes) or via agreed electronic communication.

### 3.2 Equals Money Cards

The Club uses an Equals Money prepaid account with individual virtual (and physical if needed) cards.

Cards may be issued to:

- Chairman
- Treasurer
- Secretary
- IER Manager
- Events Manager

Cardholders are responsible for ensuring all spend complies with this policy.

## 4. Approval of Expenditure

All expenditure requires prior Committee approval.

The Committee may pre‑approve budgets e.g. annual events budget or IER budget up to an agreed limit (£X).

Individual purchases within an approved budget do not require separate approval.

Any spend outside or exceeding an approved budget must be re‑approved by the Committee.

The Equals Money cards are prepaid so the approval process will include transferring the relevant balance to the individual cards.

## 5. Method of Payment

The preferred method of payment is via the Equals Money cards.

Wherever possible, purchases should be invoiced to Fenland Aero Club with the airfield billing address to ensure VAT can be reclaimed.

Where Club expenditure is incurred through online retail platforms (including Amazon) that are unable to issue invoices in the Club's name due to consumer‑based account billing, the use of an authorised Fenland Aero Club Equals Money card constitutes evidence that the purchase was approved, funded, and incurred on behalf of the Club. Such expenditure will be treated as Club expenditure provided it is wholly and exclusively for Club purposes and is supported by appropriate order documentation.

Where this is not practical, personal payment is permitted for small amounts, subject to reimbursement rules below.

## 6. Receipts, Invoices and VAT

All purchases must be supported by a valid receipt or invoice.

Card receipts or order confirmations without supplier and VAT details are not sufficient.

## 7. Submission to Xero

All invoices and receipts must be submitted promptly.

Invoices and receipts should be forwarded as attachments (preferably pdf) to the Club's Xero bills inbox address:

> *(Xero bills inbox address — held separately by the Treasurer; not stored in this repository)*

This automatically creates a bill in our accounting software Xero.

## 8. Reimbursements

Reimbursements will only be made where:

- Wholly and exclusively incurred for Club purposes
- The expenditure was approved in advance
- Valid receipts or invoices are provided.

Reimbursements will be paid by bank transfer.

## 9. Non‑Allowable Expenses

All Club roles are voluntary. The Club does not normally reimburse:

- Mileage
- Meals
- Refreshments

## 10. Review and Compliance

Misuse of Club funds or payment cards may result in withdrawal of card access.

Exceptions to this policy require explicit Committee approval.

This policy will be reviewed periodically by the Committee.
